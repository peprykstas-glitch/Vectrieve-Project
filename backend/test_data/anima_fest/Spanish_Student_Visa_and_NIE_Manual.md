# SPANISH STUDENT VISA, NIE AND LEGAL CONVENIO MANUAL

## 1. LEGAL FRAMEWORK: CONVENIO DE PRÁCTICAS
Every internship arranged by Anima Fest Experience operates under a tripartite educational training agreement (*Convenio de Prácticas Formativas*) signed by:
1. The Student's Home University / Educational Institution.
2. The Host Hotel Resort in Spain.
3. Anima Fest Experience (Coordinating Agency).

The internship is purely academic and does not constitute a standard employment contract (*contrato laboral*).

## 2. EU CITIZENS VS NON-EU CITIZENS
### A. Students with EU / EEA / Swiss Passports:
- **Visa**: No visa required.
- **Duration**: Can stay for the full internship duration (up to 180 days).
- **NIE Requirement**: White NIE (*Número de Identificación de Extranjero*) is mandatory for receiving bank transfers and Spanish Social Security registration (*Seguridad Social*). Anima Fest assists with booking the *Cita Previa* at the local police station (*Policía Nacional* / *Extranjería*).

### B. Non-EU Students (Ukrainian, Turkish, Latin American, etc.):
- **Duration under 90 days**: Can travel under Schengen visa-free (Biometric passport) or standard short-stay Schengen Type C, provided the university agreement is registered.
- **Duration over 90 days (Long-term)**: Must obtain a Spanish Student Internship Visa (*Visado de Estancia por Estudios / Prácticas*) at the Spanish Embassy/Consulate in their country of residence prior to arrival.
- **Required Visa Documents**:
  1. Formal Acceptance Letter from the Hotel.
  2. Tripartite Convenio signed by the University.
  3. Proof of accommodation and financial support guaranteed by the hotel.
  4. Comprehensive international health insurance covering minimum €30,000 without deductible.
  5. Medical certificate of good health and clean criminal record certificate (apostilled and translated into Spanish).
- **NIE Validity**: The assigned NIE number is permanent and unique for life in Spain.
