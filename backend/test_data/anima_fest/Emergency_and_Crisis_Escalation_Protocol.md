# ANIMA FEST EXPERIENCE — CRISIS, MEDICAL & DISPUTE ESCALATION PROTOCOL

## 1. EMERGENCY & MEDICAL INCIDENTS
- **European Health Insurance Card (EHIC / Tarjeta Sanitaria Europea)**: EU students must register with the local public medical center (*Centro de Salud / CAP*) in their town upon arrival.
- **Private Health Insurance (Non-EU Students)**: In case of acute illness or accidents, call the 24/7 Assistance number listed on your policy certificate before visiting a private hospital (except in life-threatening emergencies where you must call Spanish Emergency Services at **112** immediately).
- **Notification**: The student or hotel HR must notify the Anima Fest Support Team within 6 hours of any hospitalization or emergency incident.

## 2. WORKPLACE DISPUTES & EXCESSIVE HOURS (OVERTIME)
- **40-Hour Limit Enforcement**: If a hotel manager or supervisor requests an intern to work beyond 40 hours per week or refuses the mandatory 2 days off:
  1. **Do NOT unilaterally abandon the workplace**: Leaving the hotel without coordinator authorization constitutes an unauthorized departure (*abandono injustificado*) and forfeits the internship certificate and housing.
  2. **Immediate Escalation Step**: Contact your assigned Anima Fest Regional Coordinator via WhatsApp or Emergency Hotline (+34 931 760 000).
  3. **Coordinator Intervention**: Anima Fest guarantees direct mediation with the Hotel General Manager within 24 business hours. If the hotel fails to rectify the schedule, Anima Fest will relocate the intern to a new partner hotel at no additional cost.

## 3. LOST PASSPORT OR IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
If a passport or NIE document is lost or stolen:
1. Immediately file a police report (*denuncia*) at the nearest *Guardia Civil* or *Policía Nacional* station.
2. Send a scanned copy of the police report to `support@animafestexperience.com`.
3. Contact the nearest Embassy or Consulate of your home country in Madrid or Barcelona to issue an emergency travel certificate (*salvoconducto*).
