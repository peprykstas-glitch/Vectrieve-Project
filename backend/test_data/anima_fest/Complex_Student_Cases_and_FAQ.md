# ANIMA FEST EXPERIENCE — COMPLEX STUDENT CASES & FAQ HANDBOOK

## Q1: What happens if a student has severe food allergies while working in the hotel?
**Policy**: All partner hotels accommodate standard dietary requirements (Vegetarian, Halal, Lactose-free, Gluten-free). Students with acute airborne or contact allergies (e.g., severe seafood/peanut anaphylaxis) must declare this during the initial application. They will be strictly placed in Front Office or Guest Relations roles, avoiding direct F&B kitchen assignments.

## Q2: Can a student take 3–5 days off during the internship to return home for university exams?
**Policy**: Yes. Students must submit an official Examination Certificate (*Certificado de Exámenes*) from their university dean to Anima Fest and Hotel HR at least 21 days in advance. The days taken will be deducted from the internship completion hours or made up at the end of the contract by mutual agreement.

## Q3: Is it possible to extend the internship from Summer to the Winter season?
**Policy**: Yes. Students placed in the Balearic Islands or Costa Brava (summer destinations ending in October) can request an internal transfer to year-round or winter partner hotels in the Canary Islands (Tenerife, Gran Canaria) or Andorra ski resorts. The extension requires signing an addendum (*Anexo de Prórroga*) to the university agreement.

## Q4: Are flight tickets paid or reimbursed by Anima Fest or the Hotel?
**Policy**: No. Travel tickets (flights, trains, ferries to islands) are entirely organized and paid for by the student. Anima Fest assists with airport transfer instructions to reach the hotel.
