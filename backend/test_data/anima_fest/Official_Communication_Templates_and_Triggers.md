# ANIMA FEST EXPERIENCE — OFFICIAL OPERATIONAL COMMUNICATION TEMPLATES & PROTOCOLS

## 1. TEMPLATE A: INTERNSHIP SELECTION & CONFIRMATION (SELECCIÓN DE PRÁCTICAS)
- **Target Audience**: Newly selected students for hotel internships in Spain.
- **Language**: Spanish / Multilingual.
- **Trigger / Action Required**: Student must respond on the same day with:
  1. Full Name (*Nombre completo*).
  2. Personal Student Code (*Código personal*).
  3. Confirmation phrase: `*Si CONFIRMO PRACTICAS*`.
- **Warning / Consequence**: If not confirmed within the allocated deadline, the placement is automatically reassigned to another candidate on the waiting list (*pérdida de la beca de prácticas*).
- **Direct Coordination Contact**: `+34 697 184 146` (Admissions & Confirmation Department).

## 2. TEMPLATE B: URGENT RESIDENCE AUTHORIZATION & WINTER BLACKOUT (AUTORIZACIÓN DE RESIDENCIA)
- **Target Audience**: Students preparing to travel within the coming days.
- **Context**: Host hotel requires all legal documentation to process the formal Spanish residence authorization (*Autorización de Residencia para Prácticas*).
- **Critical Operational Blackout Period**: 
  - The Anima Fest documentation team is strictly on vacation during **November, December, and January**.
  - All paperwork must be finalized and submitted prior to this period; no processing support is available during winter vacation months.
- **Direct Documentation Contact**: `+34 605 19 18 06`.

## 3. TEMPLATE C: 6-MONTH INTERNSHIP EXTENSION & JOB OFFER (EXTENSIÓN DE PRÁCTICAS)
- **Target Audience**: Active interns eligible to extend their contract for an additional 6 months.
- **Career Opportunity**: Potential transition into a formal permanent employment contract (*oferta laboral*) if the hotel has an open vacancy.
- **Required Information**:
  1. Trigger keyword: `*SI QUIERO EXTENDER*`.
  2. Planned start date of next internship period.
  3. Desired department.
  4. Choice between staying at current hotel company or transferring to a new location.
- **Strict Deadline**: Maximum **4 weeks** to submit all **7 required documentation files**. Past this 4-week window, extension is legally impossible.
- **Direct WhatsApp Extension Link**: `https://wa.me/34605191806?text=Hola,%20quiero%20extender%20mi%20practica`.
