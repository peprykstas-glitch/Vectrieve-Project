# ANIMA FEST EXPERIENCE — GENERAL INTERNSHIP TERMS & CONDITIONS (SEASON 2026)

## 1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW
Anima Fest Experience is an accredited international educational agency specializing in professional internships in 3*, 4*, and 5* hotel resorts across Spain (Mainland, Balearic Islands, Canary Islands).

## 2. INTERNSHIP DURATION & WORKING HOURS
- **Duration**: Minimum 3 months (90 days), maximum 6 months (180 days).
- **Working Schedule**: 35 to 40 hours per week maximum across 5 working days.
- **Mandatory Rest Days**: Exactly 2 consecutive days off per 7-day period. Under Spanish labor and educational guidelines, overtime beyond 40 hours per week is strictly forbidden for interns (*prácticas formativas*).

## 3. MONTHLY POCKET MONEY & FINANCIAL ALLOWANCE (STIPEND)
All partner hotels provide students with a mandatory monthly pocket money allowance (*bolsa de ayuda*) transferred to the student's bank account on the 5th working day of each month:
- **Costa Brava / Costa Dorada (Mainland Spain)**: €350 – €400 net per month.
- **Mallorca & Menorca (Balearic Islands)**: €450 – €500 net per month.
- **Ibiza & Formentera (Balearic Islands)**: €500 – €550 net per month (due to higher seasonal living costs).
- **Tenerife, Gran Canaria, Lanzarote (Canary Islands)**: €400 – €450 net per month.

## 4. INCLUDED BENEFITS (MANDATORY FREE PROVISION)
1. **Accommodation**: Shared staff housing (2-3 students per room of the same gender) with AC, private/shared bathroom, and laundry facilities free of charge.
2. **Meals**: 3 full meals per day (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) in the hotel staff restaurant, 7 days a week (including rest days).
3. **Uniforms**: Work uniforms are provided by the hotel. Interns must bring their own standard black comfortable non-slip shoes.
